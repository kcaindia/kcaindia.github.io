How it works

How to join

Rising Colours is a school based competition. Individual students do not register on their own, the school enrols first and every one of its students then becomes eligible.

1

The school registers first

Only School Principals, Art Teachers or School Authorities are authorised to fill the School Registration Form. Once a school is enrolled, all its students from Nursery to Class 10 become eligible to participate.

2

Brochure by mail or WhatsApp

After registration, the official brochure and participation form are shared with the school by mail or WhatsApp, with guidelines, schedules and procedures.

3

Draw, paint and shine

Students compete in their own age category for scholarships, medals and recognition of their creativity, inside their own school.

4

National visibility and recognition

Top achievers are featured on kcaindia.com, in leading newspapers and on social media.

From Nagpur to every corner of the country, one prestigious national platform for young talent.

Qualified independent judges. Fair, transparent and merit based outcomes for every child.

School and educator recognition
