About the competition

A national stage for every kind of artist

The Grand Indian Art Contest 2026 is a national level art competition organised by KCA INDIA to discover, encourage and celebrate artistic talent from Nursery to Professional Artists across India. Participants showcase their creativity through original artworks based on category specific themes and compete for prestigious national awards and recognition.

◉Open to all India

Anyone from Nursery to Professional Artists may enter, based on the eligibility of each category.

✦10 competition categories

A dedicated category and theme for every age group and every skill level.

⚖Two level expert jury

An online screening followed by a final review of the original artwork.

★National recognition

Featured on the KCA website, digital magazine, social media and news media.

₹Cash prizes and trophies

Cash prize, gold medal, silver coin and grand memento for every category winner.

✉Certificates for everyone

Every registered participant receives a digital participation certificate and a gift coupon.

Two simple steps

How to participate

1

Register online

Complete the online registration form and pay the registration fee applicable to your category, then attach your payment screenshot inside the form.

Open the registration form

2

Upload your artwork

After successful registration and fee verification, your Registration ID and the Artwork Submission Participation Form are sent to your WhatsApp number or email. Keep the Registration ID safe, it is required in the form. Upload a clear JPG, JPEG, PNG or PDF of your artwork before the submission deadline.

Artwork upload guidelines

  • Clear front photograph of the artwork, shot in good light
  • The entire artwork must be fully visible inside the frame
  • No fingers or hands covering any part of the artwork
  • High resolution image only, no blur and no filters
  • JPG, JPEG, PNG or PDF format only, maximum file size 10 MB
  • A Registration ID is sent by WhatsApp or email once payment is verified

How winners are chosen

Two level jury evaluation

Level 1

Online screening

Participants upload a clear JPG, JPEG, PNG or PDF of their artwork inside the participation form. Expert judges evaluate every entry and shortlist the top 50 participants from each category.

Rules and regulations

1

Only original handmade artwork is accepted.

2

Only one artwork is permitted per participant.

3

Artwork must be based on the prescribed topic and prepared on the prescribed sheet or canvas and size.

4

AI generated, copied, traced, digitally created or plagiarised artwork will be rejected.

5

KCA INDIA reserves the right to disqualify copied, duplicate or ineligible entries.

6

Digital editing is not permitted, except basic brightness adjustment and cropping.

7

The uploaded artwork must exactly match the original artwork and must be clear, high resolution and unedited.

8

Only shortlisted participants must submit their original artwork, on or before 30 December 2026.

9

Failure to submit the original artwork within the prescribed timeline results in disqualification.

10

The registration fee is non refundable and non transferable under any circumstances.

11

The jury's decision shall be final and binding on all participants.

12

KCA INDIA reserves the right to amend the schedule, rules, awards or judging process whenever necessary.

Quick answers

Frequently asked questions

Who can participate?

Anyone from Nursery to Professional Artists across India, based on the eligibility of each category.

Can I submit more than one artwork?

No, only one artwork is permitted per participant.

Can I edit my artwork photograph after submission?

No digital editing is allowed, except basic brightness adjustment and cropping.

Will everyone receive a certificate?

Yes, every registered participant receives a digital participation certificate.

Can I change my category later?

No, the registration fee and the category are non refundable and non transferable.

How are winners selected?

Through a two level expert jury evaluation, an online screening followed by a final review of the original artwork.

How do awards reach the winners?

Awards for school, college and institute participants are sent to their respective institution. Awards for hobby and professional artists are sent by Speed Post to their registered postal address.

What happens to my original artwork?

Participants must preserve their original artwork safely until the competition is officially completed, even if they are not shortlisted.
