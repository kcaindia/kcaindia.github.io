Four simple steps

How to become a member

1

Membership form

The Parent or Guardian, or the member where applicable, completes the KCA International Art Club Membership Form. The official KCA International Art Club WhatsApp Community link is provided inside the form.

Open the KIAC Membership Form

2

Membership fee

Pay the annual membership fee applicable to your country. India ₹499 a year, UAE AED 25 a year, other international countries US$8 a year.

3

Membership activation

After successful registration and payment verification, KCA sends your Digital KCA International Art Club Membership Card and the Official KCA Artwork Submission Form to your registered email ID. A unique Membership ID or Entry ID is issued, and the same ID can be used for future yearly renewals and Art Club activities.

4

Join the WhatsApp Community

Join the official KCA International Art Club WhatsApp Community using the link in the Membership Form. The Community carries art activity information, submission deadlines, Artists of the Month announcements and Art Club updates.

Monthly art activity

A new theme every month, of your own choice

Monthly art theme of your own choice, every month, as per your category.

🖼️Theme

A different drawing, painting or art theme every month. Create artwork based on your desired theme.

📄Artwork size

A3 size paper is recommended. Another suitable size may be used according to your artwork and convenience.

🖌️Medium

Use suitable drawing and painting materials according to the theme.

📤Online submission

Submit your artwork online through the official KCA Artwork Submission Form received by email after completing your membership.

⏰ Monthly deadline 20th of every month Upload your artwork by the announced deadline

Artwork showcase

Rules, regulations and terms

A few simple things to know before you join

1

🪪 One membership, one artist. Your Membership ID is personal and renews every year with the same ID.

2

🎨 Original art only. Artwork should be the member's own creation.

3

⏰ Submit by the 20th each month to be considered that month.

4

👨‍👩‍👧 Minors join through a Parent or Guardian, who completes registration on their behalf.

5

⚖️ The judging panel's picks are final for Artists of the Month.

6

📢 Your art may be featured by KCA to celebrate your work with the community.

7

©️ Copyright stays with the artist, but KCA may reuse the artwork for club promotion.

8

1️⃣ One entry per member per month.

9

🚫 Appropriate content only. No offensive, plagiarised or AI generated artwork.

10

📅 A late or missed submission does not affect your membership, only that month's showcase.

11

🔄 KCA may update these rules. Changes are announced through the WhatsApp Community.

12

💛 Membership fees directly support the Art Club's activities and community, so they are non refundable and non transferable once paid.
